Hello, thanks for being here! I am Shantanu Kallakuri and this website is my research portfolio. If you are looking for my general fun science education page, please visit <b>[chemystery.org](https://chemystery.org)</b> where I discuss interesting science. Feedback, inquiry, criticism is always welcome! For details on my research, please refer to my <a href="https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WOuzLXA7qTDWK_YxqRZDP9MBVbMGUfX5/view?usp=sharing" title="CV" target="_blank"><b>CV</b></a>, <b>[publications](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=sQuyU90AAAAJ&hl=en)</b>, <b>[patents](https://patents.google.com/?inventor=shantanu+kallakuri&oq=shantanu+kallakuri)</b>, and <b>[book chapter](https://www.appleacademicpress.com/functionalized-engineering-materials-and-their-applications-/9781771885232)</b>.

I am an M.S. graduate in Materials science & engineering from <b>[Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University)</b>. I completed my undergraduation from <b>[BITS Pilani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BITS_Pilani)</b>, with an integrated B.E. in Chemical engineering with an M.Sc. in Chemistry. I am currently a Senior Process Engineer at <b>[Applied Materials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Materials)</b> (AMAT) in their Santa Clara headquarters, where I develop bottom-up atomic-layer epitaxy, deposition and etch processes for <b>[advanced](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_semiconductor_scale_examples)</b> node <b>[(<1.4nm)](https://markets.financialcontent.com/wral/article/tokenring-2026-1-13-the-angstrom-frontier-tsmc-and-intel-reveal-14nm-roadmaps-to-power-the-next-decade-of-ai)</b> semiconductor microchips.

My focus at AMAT is distributed across atomic-layer epitaxy <b>[(ALE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_layer_epitaxy)</b>, plasma-enhanced atomic layer deposition <b>[(PE-ALD)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_layer_deposition)</b> and atomic-layer etch <b>[(ALE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_layer_etching)</b>, for ongoing inflections in transistors (<b>[GAAFET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multigate_device)</b> and <b>[HEMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-electron-mobility_transistor)</b>) -- the tiny electrical switches that form the bedrock of all modern electronics. These efforts are geared towards extending <b>[Moore's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moore%27s_law)</b> into 3D transistor architectures through crystalline monolayer growth, pristine interfaces, defect suppression, and surface passivation.

I have previously also worked at AMAT on plasma-enhanced chemical vapor deposition <b>[(PE-CVD)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plas ma-enhanced_chemical_vapor_deposition)</b>, and reactive-ion etching <b>[(RIE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reactive-ion_etching)</b> for two applications: 1) to pattern, deposit, and etch photonic <b>[diffraction gratings](https://www.rp-photonics.com/diffraction_gratings.html)</b> (<b>[blazed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blazed_grating)</b>, <b>[holographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holographic_grating)</b>) and <b>[waveguides](https://www.rp-photonics.com/waveguides.html)</b> for AR/VR, and 2) seam-free metal deposition in memory chips (<b>[DRAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_random-access_memory)</b>, <b>[V-NAND](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flash_memory#Vertical_NAND)</b>). For details, please refer to my <b>[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=sQuyU90AAAAJ&hl=en)</b>.

Please refer to the "Research" tab for a detailed overview. While I have endeavored to gain expertise across diverse material systems, my interests now are more focused within semiconducting/dielectric/superconducting materials and their spin/charge/light physics. Please reach out if you share any mutual interests or would simply just like to talk! I love to discuss science at any time, or if you just need someone to hear you out.
